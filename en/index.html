How does Pam work?

Pam is an AI language teacher that works directly on WhatsApp. You can chat via text or audio, anytime, practicing English, Spanish, French, Italian and other languages naturally and judgment-free.

Do I need to download an app?

No! Pam works directly on WhatsApp, the app you already use every day. Just subscribe to a plan and add Pam to your contacts to start practicing.

Can I practice pronunciation via audio?

Yes! Pam understands and replies to audio messages, allowing you to train your pronunciation and listening comprehension just like in a real conversation. She also corrects your mistakes gently and motivationally.

Which languages does Pam teach?

Pam can help you practice English, Spanish, French, Italian and several other languages. You can switch between languages at any time during your conversations.
